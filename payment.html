Secure Payments

Transparent & Trusted Payment Process

At Gateway Engineers Academy, we are committed to providing a safe, secure, and transparent payment experience for every student enrolling in our classroom, online, and test batch courses.

Admission will be confirmed only after successful payment verification. Students are advised to carefully review all payment details before completing the transaction.

💳

Accepted Payments

Payments can be made using UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, Bank Transfer or Cash at the academy office.

✔️

Payment Verification

Course enrollment becomes active only after payment verification and confirmation from the academy.

📅

Installment Plans

Installment facilities, if offered for a course, must be paid within the specified due dates to continue uninterrupted access.

🧾

Payment Receipt

Students should retain payment receipts and transaction references for future verification and support.

Important Payment Guidelines

  • ✔ Admission is confirmed only after successful payment verification.
  • ✔ Fees once paid should be verified through the official academy receipt.
  • ✔ Students should ensure that payment details entered are accurate.
  • ✔ Payments must be made only through official Gateway Engineers Academy channels.
  • ✔ Delayed installment payments may temporarily suspend course access.
  • ✔ Fee structures may differ based on classroom, online, and special batch programs.
  • ✔ Students should immediately report any failed or duplicate transactions.
  • ✔ Official payment acknowledgement will be shared after successful processing.

Need Payment Assistance?

If you experience any payment issues, transaction failures, refund-related queries, or require assistance regarding course fee payments, our support team is always available to help. Please contact Gateway Engineers Academy through our official communication channels for quick assistance.
