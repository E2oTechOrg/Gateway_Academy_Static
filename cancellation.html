Cancellation & Refund Policy

At Gateway Engineers Academy, we are committed to maintaining a transparent and fair admission process. Students are requested to read this Cancellation & Refund Policy carefully before enrolling in any classroom or online course.

Our policies are designed to ensure clarity regarding admissions, fee payments, cancellations, refunds, and administrative procedures while maintaining the quality of our educational services.

Course Cancellation

Students who wish to cancel their admission should submit a written cancellation request before the commencement of the course. Cancellation requests will be reviewed according to the academy's admission policies.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds, if applicable, may be considered only for cancellations made before the commencement of the course. Registration fees, admission processing charges, and administrative expenses may be deducted from the refundable amount.

After Course Commencement

Once classroom sessions or online course access has been provided, tuition fees are generally non-refundable. Any exception will be considered solely at the discretion of the academy management.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will normally be processed within 7–10 working days through the original payment method. Any applicable bank charges or payment gateway fees may be deducted wherever applicable.

Important Information

Students are advised to review all course details, schedules, eligibility criteria, and fee structure before completing the admission process. By enrolling in any course offered by Gateway Engineers Academy, students acknowledge that they have read and agreed to this Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Need Assistance?

For cancellation requests, refund queries, or payment related assistance, please contact our admission office. Our team will be happy to assist you with the required information and support.
